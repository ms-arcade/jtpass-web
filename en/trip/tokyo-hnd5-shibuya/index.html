How this is calculated

Every on-sale pass is tried from each day (multi-day passes included); the non-overlapping combination with the largest saving is shown. Official fares and prices only.

Method: the app's suggested trip departs 2026-12-01 (Tue) at 09:00 (not a timetable) and is priced between the stations nearest each spot on the cheapest route of the fare engine's route comparison, or on a route at most ¥30 dearer with fewer transfers or a shorter time (total without a pass). Totals with a pass re-pick, for each pass and each leg, the route that costs the least with that pass (as on the route pages). The recommended plan tries starting each pass on each day and picks the cheapest combination without overlaps (one adult, IC fares).
