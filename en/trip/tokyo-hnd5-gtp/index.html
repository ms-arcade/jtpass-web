Haneda 5 days: Shimbashi base with Yokohama

Zojo-ji, Tokyo Tower, Roppongi; Yokohama (Landmark Tower, Red Brick Warehouse, Yamashita Park), Sengaku-ji; Asakusa, Skytree, Mukojima-Hyakkaen, Ueno, Ameyoko; Harajuku, Shibuya Sky, Gotoku-ji, Omotesando, Tocho, Omoide Yokocho; Tsukiji, Kiyosumi Garden, teamLab Planets. Planned on lines the Greater Tokyo Pass (5 days) covers: Tokyo Metro, Toei, Keisei, Keikyu, Tokyu and the Minatomirai Line.

Recommended: Toei One-Day Pass, Minatomirai Line One-Day Ticket, Tokyo Subway Ticket (72-hour) · total with passes ¥5,870 · saves ¥1,548 (without a pass: ¥7,418) · See the calculation

With the Greater Tokyo Pass

  • All 27 legs are covered by the pass (no extra fares).
  • Total with the pass ¥6,500 (incl. the ¥6,500 pass) · saves ¥918 vs. no pass (¥7,418)
  • That is ¥630 more than the cheapest plan (recommended passes).
