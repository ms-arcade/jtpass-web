Fares and pass prices are for reference only. Actual prices and conditions follow each railway operator's official information. Please check the official information before buying tickets or passes. “Unverified” marks values the app has not yet confirmed from official sources (for example, JR fares calculated from Wikipedia operating distances, or ticket fares used where the IC fare has not been collected). Such fares are estimates.

Why values on this page are marked “Unverified”:

  • The JR IC fare has not been collected yet; the ticket fare is used.
  • The JR special fare table has not been collected yet; the standard formula fare is used.
  • Part of the JR distance comes from a non-official source (Wikipedia).
