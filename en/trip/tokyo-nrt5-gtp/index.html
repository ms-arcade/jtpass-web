Narita 5 days: Ueno base with Yokohama

Asakusa & Skytree; Yokohama (Landmark Tower, Red Brick Warehouse, Yamashita Park); Harajuku, Shibuya Sky, Tocho; Tsukiji, teamLab Planets, Roppongi, Tokyo Tower; Ameyoko. Planned on lines the Greater Tokyo Pass (5 days) covers: Tokyo Metro, Toei, Keisei, Keikyu, Tokyu and the Minatomirai Line.

Recommended: Tokyo Metro 24-hour Ticket, みなとみらいきっぷ(品川), Tokyo Subway Ticket (48-hour) · total with passes ¥5,726 · saves ¥1,207 (without a pass: ¥6,933) · See the calculation

With the Greater Tokyo Pass

  • All 20 legs are covered by the pass (no extra fares).
  • Total with the pass ¥6,500 (incl. the ¥6,500 pass) · saves ¥433 vs. no pass (¥6,933)
  • That is ¥774 more than the cheapest plan (recommended passes).
