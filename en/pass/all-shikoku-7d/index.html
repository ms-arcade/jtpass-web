ALL SHIKOKU Rail Pass 7-day

ALL SHIKOKU Rail Pass 7日間

Key facts

PriceAdult ¥20,000 · Child ¥10,000
Validity7 days, consecutive, until the last train
Issued byJR Shikoku, etc.
Eligibility & conditionsForeign passport holders visiting Japan on a short-term stay only. JR Shikoku, Tosa Kuroshio Railway, Asa Kaigan Railway, Kotoden, Tosaden. Non-reserved seats only (reserved seats cannot be used with this pass alone)
ExchangeExchange for a physical ticket at a counter
SourceOfficial page (checked 2026-11-07) · price re-checked 2026-11-07

Coverage

All JR Shikoku, Tosa Kuroshio, Asa Kaigan, Kotoden & Tosaden lines, 7 days

How it is sold

  • Sales counters (stations, bus companies, etc.) — In Japan: Takamatsu, Matsuyama, Tokushima, Kochi and Kotohira stations; JR Shikoku Tour branches; JR Shikoku Tour Umeda branch
  • Online — See the official page
  • Overseas agents (exchange order) — Buy an exchange voucher from a partner travel agency overseas → exchange it at Takamatsu, Matsuyama, Tokushima, Kochi or Kotohira station etc. after arriving in Japan. Priced in local currency at the exchange rate when issued

Check the sales conditions on the official page before you buy.
