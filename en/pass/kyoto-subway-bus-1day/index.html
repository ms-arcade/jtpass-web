Discounts with this pass

Kyoto Subway & Bus One-Day Pass: Show the ticket printed with the date of use. At many venues only the person showing the ticket gets the offer. Official list of discounts (checked 2026-11-08)

Discounted spots in the app

  • Chion-in Temple: Chion-in: 10% off for one adult (high school and above) at each garden ticket window (group discount applied); children not eligible
  • Heian Jingu: Heian Jingū Shin'en (garden) admission: ¥50 off
  • Kodai-ji Temple: Kōdai-ji admission: ¥600→group rate ¥500
  • Nijo Castle: Nijō Castle admission: ¥100 off general admission (on the day of use only)
