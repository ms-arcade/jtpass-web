Sales counters and refund terms

How it is sold

  • Advance purchase — Can be bought in advance; no expiry (the date is printed on the day of use)
  • Same-day sale at stations and counters — Ticket machines, commuter pass offices and shops at subway stations

Discounts with this pass

Enjoy Eco Card: Show a ticket marked 「施設割引適用」 dated the day of your visit. Valid only for the ticket holder. Offers may change without notice. Official list of discounts (checked 2026-11-08)

Discounted spots in the app

  • Abeno Harukas: HARUKAS 300 (observatory) admission: 10% off the same-day admission price
  • Osaka City Museum of Fine Arts: Osaka City Museum of Fine Arts admission (planned exhibitions only): general ¥500→¥400, high school/university students ¥200→¥160
  • Osaka Castle: Osaka Castle Main Tower admission: adults ¥1,200→¥1,080, high school/university students ¥600→¥540 (junior high and under free)
  • Osaka Museum of History: Osaka Museum of History admission (permanent exhibition only): adults ¥600→¥540, high school/university students ¥400→¥360
  • Sakuya Konohana Kan: Sakuya Konohana Kan admission: adults ¥500→¥450 (junior high and under free)
  • Shitenno-ji Temple: Shitennō-ji admission: Central Precinct adults/university ¥500→¥400, high school ¥300→¥200 / Honbō Garden adults/university ¥300→¥200, elementary–high school ¥200→¥100 / Treasure House adults/university ¥500→¥400, high school ¥300→¥200
  • Tempozan Giant Ferris Wheel: Tempozan Giant Ferris Wheel fare: ¥1,000→¥900 (charged from age 3)
  • Umeda Sky Building: Umeda Sky Building Floating Garden Observatory admission: 10% off the regular price

Please note

Fares and pass prices are for reference only. Actual prices and conditions follow each railway operator's official information. Please check the official information before buying tickets or passes. JTPass is not an official app of any railway operator.

“Unverified” marks values the app has not yet confirmed from official sources (for example, JR fares calculated from Wikipedia operating distances, or ticket fares used where the IC fare has not been collected). Such fares are estimates.

Why values on this page are marked “Unverified”:

  • The JR IC fare has not been collected yet; the ticket fare is used.
  • JR special section fares have not been collected yet; the distance-based fare (operating km) is used.
  • Part of the JR distance comes from a non-official source (Wikipedia).

Line names are shown in Japanese, with the operator's English name in parentheses.
