Sales counters and refund terms

How it is sold

  • Sales counters (stations, bus companies, etc.) — Travel agencies in Japan (up to one month before the first day)
  • Online — JR-WEST ONLINE TRAIN RESERVATION (from one month before use, 10:00 JST; credit card)
  • Overseas agents (exchange order) — Travel agents outside Japan (exchange order MCO or E-TICKET; exchange within 3 months of issue)

Check the sales conditions on the official page before you buy.

Why there is no break-even calculation

  • The area where this pass is used is not yet part of the app's route network.
