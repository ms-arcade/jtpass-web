Key facts

PriceAdult ¥35,000 · Child ¥17,500
Validity5 days, consecutive, until midnight
Issued byJR East
Eligibility & conditionsForeign passport holders (foreigners living in Japan can also buy it); one pass at a time; no youth discount; children 6-11 (age on the purchase date). Not valid on the Tokaido Shinkansen, Izu Kyuko or Fujikyuko; Green Car and GranClass need extra charges

Coverage

All JR East lines (incl. Shinkansen, limited expresses and BRT; ordinary cars), Tokyo Monorail, Aoimori, IGR, Sendai Airport Transit, Sanriku, Hokuetsu Express etc., Nikko/Kinugawa through limited expresses, JR buses in the area, 5 consecutive days
