Price¥330Adult

Key facts

PriceAdult ¥330 (earns ANA miles; not a fare discount) · Child not sold
About this price: Shown: one way Haneda T1/T2 → Shinagawa (valid 1 day). One way to Sengakuji ¥370 (valid 1 day); round trip ¥660/¥740 (valid 10 days). Paid in yen; ANA miles are credited
Validity1 day, until the last train
Issued byKeikyu
Eligibility & conditionsHaneda T1/T2 → Shinagawa one way ¥330 or → Sengakuji one way ¥370 (20 ANA miles either way); round trip ¥660/¥740 (50 ANA miles either way, 10 days). Adults only; ANA Mileage Club members (when buying, insert an ANA Mileage Club card or ANA Card, or enter the 10-digit membership number). Sold only at Keikyu ticket machines at New Chitose Airport and Osaka International (Itami) Airport (both one way and round trip; not at Haneda Airport station ticket machines). Miles are credited instead of a fare discount

Sales counters and refund terms

How it is sold

  • Same-day sale at stations and counters — Only at Keikyu ticket machines installed at New Chitose Airport and Osaka International (Itami) Airport (both one way and round trip; no advance sales). Not sold at the ticket machines of Haneda Airport Terminal 1·2 and Terminal 3 stations. Refunds at Keikyu stations (except Sengakuji)

Check the sales conditions on the official page before you buy.

Why there is no break-even calculation

  • It is a ticket that earns ANA miles, not a fare discount.
