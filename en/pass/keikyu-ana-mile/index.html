京急ANAのマイルきっぷ

Key facts

PriceAdult ¥330 (earns ANA miles; not a fare discount) · Child not sold
About this price: Shown: one way Haneda T1/T2 → Shinagawa (valid 1 day). One way to Sengakuji 370 yen (valid 1 day); round trip 660/740 yen (valid 10 days). Paid in yen; ANA miles are credited
Validity1 day, until the last train
Issued byKeikyu
EligibilityHaneda T1/T2 → Shinagawa one way 330 yen, → Sengakuji 370 yen (20 miles); round trip 660/740 yen (50 miles, 10 days). Adults only; ANA Mileage Club members (when buying, insert an ANA Mileage Club card or ANA Card, or enter the 10-digit membership number). Sold only at Keikyu ticket machines at New Chitose Airport and Osaka International (Itami) Airport (both one way and round trip; not at Haneda Airport station ticket machines). Miles are credited instead of a fare discount
SourceOfficial page (checked 2026-11-07) · price re-checked 2026-11-07

Coverage

Keikyu Haneda Airport (T1/T2)→Shinagawa/Sengakuji one way or return + ANA miles

Source: www.keikyu.co.jp (checked 2026-11-07)

Where to buy

How it is sold

  • Same-day sale at stations and counters — Only at Keikyu ticket machines installed at New Chitose Airport and Osaka International (Itami) Airport (both one way and round trip; no advance sales). Not sold at the ticket machines of Haneda Airport Terminal 1·2 and Terminal 3 stations. Refunds at Keikyu stations (except Sengakuji)

Check the sales conditions on the official page before you buy.

Why there is no break-even calculation

  • It is a ticket that earns ANA miles, not a fare discount.

Price, coverage and where to buy on this page summarize the official information.
