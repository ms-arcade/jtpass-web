City Bus & Subway 1-Day Ticket

バス・地下鉄全線一日乗車券

Key facts

PriceAdult ¥870 · Child ¥430
Validity1 day, until the last train
Issued byTransportation Bureau City of Nagoya
Eligibility & conditionsAll subway lines + city buses and the Me-guru Nagoya sightseeing route bus (Meitetsu buses excluded). From the first to the last train. Tickets bought from ticket machines or on buses are valid only on the day of purchase
SourceOfficial page (checked 2026-11-08)

Coverage

All Nagoya City Subway lines and city buses, one day

Source: www.kotsu.city.nagoya.jp (checked 2026-11-08)

Where to buy

Official purchase and sales links

  • Where to buy (official) (checked 2026-11-08)

How it is sold

  • Same-day sale at stations and counters — Transportation Bureau service centers, station offices, manaca-compatible ticket machines, gate counters and retail outlets, and on city buses

Check the sales conditions on the official page before you buy.

Discounts with this pass

Nagoya day-ticket offers (Nagoya Toku-Navi): Show a started, still-valid 1-day or 24-hour ticket when entering. Tickets without a printed validity date do not qualify. Generally one person per ticket; cannot be combined with other discounts. Official list of discounts (checked 2026-11-08)

Discounted spots in the app

  • Atsuta Jingu: Atsuta Jingū Treasure Hall: adults ¥500→¥400, elementary/junior high ¥200→¥100 / Kusanagi Hall (Sword Treasury): adults ¥500→¥400, elementary/junior high ¥200→¥100 / combined ticket: adults ¥800→¥700, elementary/junior high ¥300→¥200 (group rate for special/planned exhibitions)
  • Nagoya Castle: Nagoya Castle admission: adults ¥1,000→¥800 (junior high and under free)

Why there is no break-even calculation

  • The area where this pass is used is not yet part of the app's route network.
  • It includes bus rides, and the app's data has no bus routes.

Price, coverage and where to buy on this page summarize the official information.
